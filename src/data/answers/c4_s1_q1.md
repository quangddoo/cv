# Career Narrative — Chuẩn bị Câu chuyện Sự nghiệp

## ⚡ Tóm tắt ngắn (30s)
* **Career narrative** = bài giới thiệu 2-3 phút về sự nghiệp, thể hiện tính kế thừa + định hướng.
* **Structure**:
  - **Domain expertise**: lĩnh vực sâu (fintech, e-com, SaaS).
  - **Ownership growth**: junior → IC → tech lead.
  - **Scale challenges**: từ MVP → millions of users.
  - **Reliability/Quality focus**: trầm tích chứ không chỉ ship.
  - **Impact seeking**: tìm role với impact rõ.
* **Adapt** theo company: highlight relevant.

---

## 🔍 Template

```
"I'm [name], with [N] years experience in [domain].

Most recently at [company], I [accomplishment].

Before that, I [previous role + transition].

My strength is [technical depth area] — I've handled [scale/reliability example].

I'm looking for a role where I can [specific impact] — which is why I'm interested in [this company]."
```

---

## 💻 Filled example

```
"I'm Quang, with 8 years in backend engineering, focused on fintech and high-throughput systems.

At ABC Bank, I led the payments platform team — we processed 5M transactions/day with 99.99% uptime. My biggest win: redesigning the ledger from sync 2PC to event-sourcing Saga, which reduced p99 latency from 800ms to 120ms.

Before that, I was Senior at XYZ where I built fraud detection — ML model serving with sub-100ms scoring on 10M transactions/day.

My strength is reliability at scale — I've handled hundreds of incidents, designed systems with proper observability, and mentored 5 senior engineers in my last team.

I'm looking for a role where I can shape architecture for a high-stakes domain, particularly enterprises moving to event-driven. That's why your team at [Company] caught my attention."
```

## ⚠️ Common Mistakes & Pro Tips

1. **Too long**: 2-3 min, not 10.
2. **Resume read**: don't recite CV; tell story.
3. **No quantification**: vague metrics.
4. **No "why"**: missing motivation.
5. **Generic ending**: tailor to company.

**Pro tip**: practice telling out loud 10+ times. Refine until smooth.
