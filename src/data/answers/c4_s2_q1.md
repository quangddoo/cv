# CV Walkthrough — Per-Project Walkthrough

## ⚡ Tóm tắt ngắn (30s)
* **Mỗi project trong CV**: chuẩn bị walkthrough 3-5 min trả lời:
  - **Scale**: users, QPS, data volume.
  - **Team size**: who you worked with.
  - **Your responsibility**: specific role.
  - **Difficulty**: hardest challenge.
  - **Result**: quantifiable outcome.
* **Mục đích**: when interviewer asks "Tell me about [project]", you have ready story.

---

## 💻 Template per project

```markdown
# Project: [Name in CV]

## Scale
- Users: X
- QPS: Y
- Data: Z TB

## Team
- Size: N engineers.
- My role: [Tech Lead / Senior IC / IC].
- Reporting to: [Engineering Manager].

## My responsibility
- Specific 2-3 areas.
- Key decisions made.

## Hardest challenge
- Problem description.
- Why difficult.
- Approach + result.

## Result
- Metric improvements.
- Business impact.

## What I'd do differently
- 1-2 lessons.
```

---

## 💻 Example fill-in

```markdown
# Project: Payment Platform Refactor

## Scale
- 5M tx/day, peak 10k TPS.
- 200GB transactions/year.
- 99.99% SLA.

## Team
- 6 engineers, I was Tech Lead.
- Reported to EM.

## Responsibility
- Architecture decision (event-sourcing).
- Performance optimization.
- Incident response lead.

## Hardest challenge
- Migrating from sync 2PC to Saga without downtime.
- Required dual-write, shadow tests, gradual cutover over 4 months.
- Solved by: feature flag rollout + reconciliation job.

## Result
- p99 latency 800ms → 120ms.
- Cost saved $200k/year (less DB load).
- Zero downtime.

## What I'd do differently
- Invest more in saga observability before migration.
- More integration tests for compensations.
```

## ⚠️ Common Mistakes & Pro Tips

1. **No prep**: "Um, that was 2 years ago...".
2. **Vague scale**: "lots of traffic" → specific numbers.
3. **Group success**: "we did" → "I did".
4. **Skip difficulty**: easy projects boring.

**Pro tip**: rehearse 2-3 strongest projects. Easier to navigate to them than recall obscure ones.
