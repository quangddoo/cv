# Reverse Questions — 10 Câu hỏi cho Interviewer Thể hiện Ownership

## ⚡ Tóm tắt ngắn (30s)
* **Mục đích**: showcase senior mindset + assess company. Don't waste this slot.
* **Categories**:
  1. **Architecture & Tech**: depth of role.
  2. **Team & Culture**: how Senior is supported.
  3. **Growth**: trajectory + learning.
  4. **Challenges**: real problems you'd solve.
* **Avoid**: "What's perks?", "How's WLB?" (use Glassdoor first).
* **Best questions are SPECIFIC, show research, reveal Senior thinking.**

---

## 🔍 10 high-quality questions

### Technical depth
1. **"What's the architecture decision team most debates? Why?"**
   - Reveals genuine tension areas.
2. **"What's your tech debt strategy? Concrete examples?"**
   - See if engineering valued vs feature-only.
3. **"How do you measure engineering productivity?"**
   - DORA metrics? Lines of code? Beware.

### Team & process
4. **"How does team handle production incident?"**
   - Blameless? Postmortem? On-call rotation?
5. **"What's the code review culture?"**
   - LGTM-rubber-stamp vs deep review.
6. **"How does Senior contribute to mentoring junior?"**
   - Concrete expectation.

### Growth
7. **"What does promotion look like from senior → staff?"**
   - Realistic timeline + criteria.
8. **"How does company invest in conference, training?"**
   - Budget? Time?

### Challenges
9. **"What's a problem the team is stuck on right now?"**
   - Real depth.
10. **"If I joined, what would my first 3 months focus on?"**
    - Sets expectation, shows preparedness.

## ⚠️ Common Mistakes & Pro Tips

1. **"Tell me about the company"**: lazy, read website first.
2. **"Is there overtime?"**: better via Glassdoor.
3. **"What's salary?"**: end-stage, with recruiter.
4. **Generic questions**: show no research.
5. **Too many questions**: 2-4 per round enough.

**Pro tip**: prepare 5-7 questions, pick best for situation. Listen to interview to refine question.

**Strong signal**: ask questions that build on what they said. E.g. "You mentioned X earlier — how does team handle Y for X?".
