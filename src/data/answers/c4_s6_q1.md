# Leadership Stories — Mentor, Code Review, Conflict, Incident

## ⚡ Tóm tắt ngắn (30s)
* **Mục đích**: Senior interviewer always asks about leadership. Prepare 4-6 stories:
  1. **Mentor junior** to senior promotion.
  2. **Code review** — saw issue, gave good feedback.
  3. **Technical design dispute** — disagreed with team, resolved.
  4. **Conflict with PM/stakeholder** — pushed back constructively.
  5. **Incident priority** — led incident response.
  6. **Hiring decision** — when you made the call.
* **Format**: CCO-DRL like Story Bank.

---

## 💻 Example: Mentoring story

```markdown
# Story: Mentored junior to senior in 2 years

## Context
At ABC Co, Junior engineer Bob joined 2022. Solid coder but no architectural thinking.

## Problem
Tech Lead role coming up but Bob wasn't ready.

## Constraint
- Tight timeline (1 year).
- I was Tech Lead at same level.
- Bob's manager not engaged in growth.

## Options
1. Push Bob into bigger projects without scaffolding.
2. Pair on every design — high time investment.
3. Structured mentoring program.

## Decision
Chose structured mentoring:
- Weekly 1:1, 1h.
- Each quarter: stretch project Bob owns end-to-end.
- I review PRs deeply, explain "why" not just "what".
- Brought Bob to design reviews of cross-team projects.

## Result
- 18 months later, Bob promoted Tech Lead.
- Took over my project when I changed team.
- Bob mentored next junior similarly.

## Lesson
- Mentoring scales when mentee owns.
- Stretch project > theoretical training.
- Show "why" — most valuable.
```

## 💻 Example: Conflict story

```markdown
# Story: Pushed back PM on feature scope

## Context
PM wanted new ML model in production within 1 month.

## Problem
Model untested at scale + no monitoring planned.

## Constraint
- Sales had committed to client.
- Tight deadline.

## Decision
- Met PM with concrete risks: model has 12% error rate; no rollback plan.
- Proposed: launch with limited cohort + monitoring; expand if metric good.
- Offered to invest 1 week extra setting up monitoring.

## Result
- PM agreed phased rollout.
- 1st cohort showed 18% error → model regressed.
- Saved client churn estimated $200k.

## Lesson
- Don't just say no — bring alternative.
- Quantify risk in their terms (revenue, customers).
- Build trust before crisis.
```

## ⚠️ Common Mistakes & Pro Tips

1. **No conflict story**: interviewer assumes you avoid hard conversations.
2. **All wins**: have a failure leadership story too.
3. **Vague impact**: quantify (promoted, $X saved, churn avoided).
4. **No "I"**: be specific about YOUR role.
5. **Generic "I helped team"**: nondescript = no signal.

**Pro tip**: leadership stories distinguish Senior from mid-level. Be ready with at least 3.
